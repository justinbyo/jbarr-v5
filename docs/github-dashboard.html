Product + Design by Justin Barr Young

Case Study

GitHub Navigation Redesign

Scaling the dashboard UI for 100 million developers

Company

GitHub

Role

Product Manager, UI Platform

Year

2022–2023

Outcome

Shipped to 100M+ users

Overview

GitHub’s navigation had remained largely unchanged for years, even as the product grew dramatically in breadth and complexity. As PM for GitHub’s UI Platform team, I led the end-to-end redesign of the global navigation—touching every page on GitHub.com and used by more than 100 million developers worldwide.

Problem

The existing navigation was built for a simpler era of GitHub. As the product expanded to include Discussions, Codespaces, Projects, and more, the nav struggled to surface new areas without becoming cluttered. Paths were inconsistent across personal, organization, and repository contexts, and accessibility gaps needed to be closed to honor GitHub’s commitment to all developers.

Approach

I partnered with design and engineering leads to frame the right problem before moving to solutions. We conducted discovery research across user segments—from students to enterprise admins—and ran internal listening sessions to synthesize pain points. Four principles guided the work:

  • Scalable by design: accommodate future product areas without constant rework.
  • Accessible from day one: full keyboard navigation, screen-reader support, and WCAG AA compliance built in, not bolted on.
  • Context-aware: adapt gracefully to personal, organization, and repository contexts.
  • Familiar, not foreign: preserve the mental models of millions of existing developers.

We iterated on prototypes using Primer, GitHub’s design system, ensuring new components were reusable across the product surface.

My role

As PM I owned the product strategy and roadmap, wrote specifications, and drove cross-functional alignment across design, engineering, and accessibility teams. I managed the phased rollout and led the beta program, coordinating with marketing and documentation to announce the public beta in June 2023.

Result

The redesigned navigation launched in public beta in June 2023 and was rolled out globally to all GitHub users. It introduced a persistent, collapsible sidebar that scales cleanly across contexts, meaningfully improved keyboard and screen-reader accessibility, and provides a solid foundation for future dashboard evolution.
